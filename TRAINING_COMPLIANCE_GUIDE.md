## 1. Onboarding Process

### Timeline: 90 Days

#### Week 1: Orientation
**Responsibility:** Human Resources Department

**Day 1:**
- Welcome and introduction to company culture
- Facility tour of assigned location
- IT equipment issuance (laptop, badge, phone)
- Accounts setup (email, network, VPN)
- Benefits overview enrollment session
- Completion of I-9 and tax forms

**Days 2-5:**
- Department introduction
- Role-specific training overview
- Workspace setup
- Initial compliance training:
  - Code of Conduct (1 hour)
  - HIPAA Privacy 101 (2 hours)
  - Data Security Basics (1.5 hours)
  - Anti-Fraud & Abuse (1 hour)

#### Weeks 2-4: Functional Training
**Responsibility:** Department Manager & Team Lead

**Billing Specialists:**
- Billing software systems overview (CPT/ICD codes)
- Insurance verification process
- Claims submission procedures
- Denial management workflows
- Quality standards and accuracy requirements
- Customer service protocols

**Administrative Staff:**
- Microsoft Office suite proficiency
- Company systems navigation
- Document management procedures
- Communication protocols

**Technology Staff:**
- System architecture overview
- Infrastructure and security protocols
- Network access management
- Disaster recovery procedures
- Change management process

**Compliance Staff:**
- Regulatory landscape overview
- Internal policy review
- Audit procedures
- Documentation standards

#### Weeks 5-12: Shadow & Practice
- Paired work with experienced team member (2 weeks)
- Independent work with supervision (2 weeks)
- Performance checkpoint meeting

#### Week 13: Probationary Review
- Formal 90-day review with manager
- Competency assessment
- Q&A and feedback session
- Final sign-off by HR

## 3. HIPAA Compliance

### HIPAA Privacy Rule

**Who it applies to:** All employees who handle Protected Health Information (PHI)

**What is PHI?** Any health information that can identify an individual, including:
- Patient names
- Medical record numbers
- Diagnosis codes
- Billing information
- Insurance details
- Dates of service
- Prescriptions

### Privacy Protection Requirements

**Do's:**
✓ Use secure, encrypted channels for PHI communication
✓ Limit access to only "need to know" basis
✓ Use patient identifiers cautiously
✓ Report PHI on secure platforms only
✓ Lock PHI documents when not in use
✓ Log out of systems when away from desk

**Don'ts:**
✗ Do not discuss PHI publicly or in hallways
✗ Do not forward PHI via unsecured email
✗ Do not share login credentials
✗ Do not take PHI off company premises without approval
✗ Do not leave PHI visible on desk or computer screen
✗ Do not post identifiable information on social media

### Reporting Security Breaches

**Immediate Actions (within 2 hours):**
1. Contact your manager or supervisor
2. Notify IT Security team: security@medcarebilling.com
3. Do not alert the patient or client yet

**IT Response:**
- Breach assessment and containment
- System analysis and forensics
- Notification letter drafting (if required)

**Timeline:**
- Breaches affecting <10 individuals: Internal review only
- Breaches affecting 10-499 individuals: Notification within 30 days
- Breaches affecting 500+ individuals: Notification + regulatory filing within 60 days

**Penalties for violations:**
- First offense: Minimum $100-$1,000 per violation with annual maximum $500,000
- Subsequent offenses: Increased penalties + possible criminal charges
- Termination for intentional violations

## 4. Data Security Standards

### Password Management

**Requirements:**
- Minimum 12 characters
- Mixed case (uppercase & lowercase)
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Changed every 90 days
- Never shared or written down
- Never reuse previous 5 passwords

**Examples of STRONG passwords:**
- M3dCare2024!Billing
- SecureData#Healthcare18
- MedBill$Safe2024

**Examples of WEAK passwords:**
- Password123
- Medcare2024
- 123456789
- ABCDEFGH

### Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

**Required for:**
- VPN access
- Email access (from outside office)
- Financial systems
- HR/Payroll systems
- Client portals

**How it works:**
1. Enter username and password
2. Receive code via authenticator app or SMS
3. Enter code to complete login
4. Access granted

### Device Security

**Company-Issued Devices:**
- Never share with other people
- Enable automatic lock (5-minute timeout)
- Keep antivirus/malware protection active
- Update software and patches monthly
- Report lost/stolen devices immediately

**Personal Device Policy:**
- Use of personal devices requires written approval
- Must have password protection
- Must have remote wipe capability enabled
- Automatic security updates required
- No installation of unapproved software

### Network & VPN Usage

**VPN is required when:**
- Working remotely
- Using public WiFi (coffee shop, airport, etc.)
- Accessing company systems outside the office
- Handling any PHI

**VPN Connection Procedure:**
1. Install VPN client (IT sets up)
2. Launch VPN application
3. Enter username and password
4. Complete MFA authentication
5. Wait for "Connected" status
6. Access company resources

### Email Security

**Secure attachment sharing:**
- Use EncryptionManager for emails with PHI
- Maximum attachment size: 25 MB
- File types allowed: PDF, XLSX, DOCX, TXT (encrypted)
- Scanning occurs: Automatically on send

**Red flags for phishing:**
- Suspicious sender address
- Urgent language ("immediate action required")
- Requests for passwords or sensitive info
- Links to unfamiliar sites
- Grammar/spelling errors
- Threats or unusual requests

**If you suspect phishing email:**
1. DO NOT click any links
2. DO NOT download attachments
3. Forward to security@medcarebilling.com
4. Delete the email
5. Report to your manager

## 5. Quality Assurance Program

### Accuracy Standards by Role

**Billing Specialists:** 98% accuracy required
- Audit frequency: 10% of monthly claims (minimum)
- Scoring criteria: Correct coding, proper documentation, timely submission

**Hospital Billing Specialists:** 97% accuracy required
- Audit frequency: 15% of monthly claims
- Scoring criteria: Claim correctness, denial prevention, compliance

**Lab Billing Specialists:** 98.5% accuracy required
- Audit frequency: 10% of monthly claims
- Scoring criteria: Code selection, documentation, regulatory compliance

### QA Audit Process

**Monthly Audits:**
1. Random selection of 10-15% of work
2. Blind review by QA analyst
3. Scoring against accuracy rubric
4. Feedback report generated
5. Results shared with employee and manager

**Performance Thresholds:**
- Above 98%: Excellent - Recognition and potential advancement
- 95-98%: Acceptable - Requires improvement plan
- Below 95%: Unacceptable - Performance improvement plan (60 days)
- Persistent failures: Termination

### Feedback & Improvement

**Weekly Check-ins:**
- Q&A analyst meets with employee
- Reviews audit findings
- Discusses improvements
- Provides coaching

**Monthly Reviews:**
- Performance trend analysis
- Comparison to team standards
- Recognition for improvements
- Development planning

**Quarterly Reviews:**
- Comprehensive performance assessment
- Goal setting for next quarter
- Career development discussion
- Compensation bonus eligibility review

## 7. Continuing Education Requirements

### Annual Hour Requirements by Role

**Billing Specialists:** 20 CEU hours/year
**Senior Specialists:** 25 CEU hours/year
**Managers:** 15 CEU hours/year (professional development)
**Directors & Above:** 10 CEU hours/year (executive education)

### Approved CE Providers

1. **AAPC** (American Association of Professional Coders)
   - ICD-10 updates
   - Medical coding specialties
   - Compliance topics

2. **AHIMA** (American Health Information Management Association)
   - Health information management
   - Coding and documentation
   - Compliance and audit

3. **Company Internal Training**
   - System updates and certification
   - Policy and procedure updates
   - Specialized billing topics

4. **Healthcare Conferences**
   - AAPC Annual Conferences
   - AHIMA General Assembly
   - Industry-specific seminars

### CE Documentation

**Required documentation:**
- Certificate of completion or transcript
- Date completed
- Topics covered
- Number of hours (CEU or CME)
- Provider name and ID

**Submission:**
- Maintain copy in personnel file
- Report to HR by December 31
- Include in annual performance review
- Verification for annual compliance check

**Failure to meet CE requirements:**
- Written warning (first quarter not met)
- Performance improvement plan (second quarter not met)
- Possible termination (third quarter not met)
