# MedCare Billing Solutions - Company Overview

## About MedCare Billing Solutions

**Founded:** 2015
**Headquarters:** Atlanta, Georgia, USA
**Employees:** 450+
**Industry:** Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management & Medical Billing Services

### Mission
To streamline healthcare billing operations for hospitals and diagnostic laboratories through innovative technology and expert service delivery.

### Core Services

1. **Hospital Billing Management**
   - Patient account management
   - Insurance claim processing
   - Payment posting and reconciliation
   - Denials management
   - Revenue optimization

2. **Laboratory Billing Services**
   - Test coding and documentation
   - Insurance verification
   - Result delivery and billing
   - Laboratory compliance support

3. **Accounts Receivable Management**
   - Patient payment collection
   - Bad debt recovery
   - Collections management
   - Financial reporting

4. **Consulting Services**
   - Billing audit and optimization
   - Workflow design
   - Staff training
   - Compliance consulting
